# Licensing Architecture for The Foundation

The Foundation uses a clear, multi-part open-source and open-knowledge licensing framework:

---

## 1. Software Code
All software code in this repository (Python packages, CLI tools, calculation engines, data parsers, build scripts, tests, web application HTML/CSS/JS, and GitHub Actions workflows) is licensed under the **[Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE)**.

```text
SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0
```

---

## 2. Methodology, Research, & Documentation
All economic methodology specifications, formulas, research papers, design records, decision logs, and narrative documentation (including `METHODOLOGY.md`, `PRD.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `VALIDATION.md`, and site explanatory texts) are licensed under the **[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**.

```text
SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0
```

### Attribution Requirements
When citing, redistributing, or adapting The Foundation's methodology, attribution must be provided as follows:
> *Methodology by The Foundation (https://github.com/sumosizedginger/foundation), licensed under CC BY 4.0.*

---

## 3. Precomputed Data & Validation Outputs
All precomputed data artifacts (`data/current/*.json`, `data/history/*.json`, `data/metadata/*.json`) generated by the deterministic pipeline are made available under **[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)** to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, subject to the retention of primary source attribution from original statistical agencies (U.S. Census Bureau, U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, U.S. Department of Agriculture).

---

## 4. Brand Identity & Trademarks
The project name ("The Foundation"), logos, marks, and design emblems are explicitly reserved and are **not** licensed under Apache 2.0 or CC BY 4.0. You may not use the marks to imply endorsement or official affiliation without prior written consent.

# Context

## Why this exists

Conventional economic headlines often emphasize aggregate output, financial markets, aggregate household wealth, and headline unemployment.

Those measures are useful but can coexist with serious deterioration among people who have the least financial margin.

The Foundation asks a deliberately different first question:

> How are the people holding up the economic foundation doing?

The project is based on a building analogy:

- A healthy roof does not prove the foundation is sound.
- Aggregate prosperity does not prove broad economic security.
- A system that produces more while making ordinary life increasingly inaccessible deserves a measurement framework capable of showing that divergence.

## Bottom 30% as a reference point

The "Bottom 30%" is not a moral label and is not synonymous with "the poor."

It is a population slice chosen to establish a consistent reference point for evaluating:

- work access;
- cost pressure;
- housing;
- resilience;
- ownership;
- mobility.

The project should show improvements with the same force that it shows deterioration.

It is not designed to prove that the United States is failing.

It is designed to find out.

## Household philosophy

Raw household income is misleading when households support different numbers of humans.

For this project:

```text
$80,000 supporting 4 people
```

is not treated as equivalent to:

```text
$80,000 supporting 1 person.
```

The canonical V0.1 ranking therefore divides household money income by the number of people in the household.

No fractional-person equivalence scale is used.

## Economic philosophy

The project does not require:

- socialism;
- communism;
- laissez-faire capitalism;
- anti-capitalism;
- partisan allegiance.

Its measurement premise is narrower:

> An economy should be judged in meaningful part by whether people with the least economic room can establish stable lives and improve their position.

The instrument should remain useful to people who disagree about the policy solution.

## Tone versus methodology

The public project may eventually have a distinctive editorial voice.

The data layer does not.

Calculations must be boring, documented, reproducible, and hostile to convenient assumptions.

## What the agent should understand

The owner is intentionally using agentic development to avoid manual coding.

That does **not** mean the agent owns methodological judgment.

The owner sets the meaning of the instrument.

The agent implements and tests it.

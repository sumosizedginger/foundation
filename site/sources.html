SOURCE REGISTRY & PROVENANCE

Primary sources first.

Production data come directly from original government statistical publishers. Aggregators are used solely for discovery and cross-checking.

Registered Source Connectors

U.S. CENSUS BUREAU · CPS ASEC & ACS CANONICAL ANCHOR & WEIGHTS

Current Population Survey (ASEC) & American Community Survey

Primary microdata archive used to compute person-ranked household income percentiles (P10–P90), the Bottom-30 Population Anchor, and county-level adult population weights.

Variables: HTOTVAL · H_NUMPER · MARSUPWT · H_SEQ · A_LINENO · B01001_001E · B01001_007E-025E
Cadence: Annual (March Supplement & 5-Year ACS) · Universe: Civilian Noninstitutional Population
Official Dataset: census.gov/cps-asec Verified (2023, 2024, 2025)
U.S. BUREAU OF LABOR STATISTICS PRESSURE SIGNALS & EXPENDITURES

BLS Public API v2 & Consumer Expenditure Survey (CE)

High-frequency monthly labor underutilization, employment attachment, CPI sub-indices, plus single-person necessities, vehicle maintenance, and recreation expenditures.

Series IDs: LNS13327709 (U-6) · LNS11300000 (Participation) · LNS12300000 (Emp-Pop) · LNS15026639 (Want Job) · CUSR0000SA0 (CPI) · CUSR0000SAH1 (Shelter) · CUSR0000SAF11 (Food) · CUSR0000SAM (Medical) · CUSR0000SETB01 (Gasoline)
Cadence: Monthly & Annual · Endpoint: api.bls.gov/publicAPI/v2/timeseries/data/
Official Portal: bls.gov/developers 9 Series Ingested
U.S. DEPT. OF HOUSING & URBAN DEVELOPMENT (HUD) HOUSING BASELINE

Fair Market Rents (FMR) 1-Bedroom 40th Percentile

Used to establish independent 1-Bedroom rental housing costs across all ~3,143 U.S. counties and FMR areas for FY2024 and FY2026.

Official Portal: huduser.gov/fmr 50 States + DC Coverage
U.S. DEPT. OF AGRICULTURE (USDA) FOOD BASELINE

USDA Low-Cost & Thrifty Food Plans

Nutritional cost baseline for home-prepared meals for single adults age 19–50 with official +20% 1-person size adjustments and AK/HI factors.

Official Portal: fns.usda.gov Low-Cost & Thrifty Reports
CMS & AHRQ MEPS HEALTHCARE BASELINE

Marketplace Public Use Files (PUF) & Medical Expenditure Panel Survey

Unsubsidized lowest-cost adequate Silver health insurance premiums for single adults age 40 plus expected non-catastrophic out-of-pocket medical utilization.

Official Portal: cms.gov/marketplace Zero Subsidies Baseline
FHWA, EIA, NAIC & BEA TRANSIT & REGIONAL PRICE PARITIES

Automobile Model & BEA Regional Price Parities (RPP)

Synthesizes FHWA NHTS mileage (~11k miles), EIA state retail gasoline prices, NAIC state auto liability insurance, and BEA RPP regional cost adjustments.

Official Portal: bea.gov/rpp Regional Adjustments

Primary sources first.

Production data come directly from original government statistical publishers. Aggregators are used solely for discovery and cross-checking.

U.S. CENSUS BUREAU · CPS ASEC CANONICAL ANCHOR

Current Population Survey — Annual Social & Economic Supplement

Primary microdata archive used to compute person-ranked household income percentiles (P10–P90) and the Bottom-30 Population Anchor.

Variables: HTOTVAL · H_NUMPER · MARSUPWT · H_SEQ · A_LINENO
Cadence: Annual (March Supplement) · Universe: Civilian Noninstitutional Population
Official Dataset: census.gov/cps-asec Verified (2023, 2024, 2025)
U.S. BUREAU OF LABOR STATISTICS PRESSURE SIGNALS

BLS Public Data API v2

High-frequency monthly labor underutilization, employment attachment, and Consumer Price Index (CPI) sub-indices.

Series IDs: LNS13327709 (U-6) · LNS11300000 (Participation) · LNS12300000 (Emp-Pop) · LNS15026639 (Want Job) · CUSR0000SA0 (CPI) · CUSR0000SAH1 (Shelter) · CUSR0000SAF11 (Food) · CUSR0000SAM (Medical) · CUSR0000SETB01 (Gasoline)
Cadence: Monthly · Endpoint: api.bls.gov/publicAPI/v2/timeseries/data/
Official Portal: bls.gov/developers 9 Series Ingested
U.S. DEPT. OF HOUSING & URBAN DEVELOPMENT (HUD) SURVIVAL BASELINE

Fair Market Rents (FMR) & ACS Gross Rent

Used to establish the 40th-percentile residential shelter cost baseline across 0BR/1BR/2BR/3BR units for the Survival Floor.

Official Portal: huduser.gov/portal/datasets/fmr.html Research Baseline

Nutritional cost baseline for home-prepared meals across age groups and household compositions with official size adjustments.

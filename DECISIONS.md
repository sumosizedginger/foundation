## D-001 — Bottom-30 denominator
**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** ACCEPTED

Household money resources are divided by the number of humans supported in the household.
V0.1 operationalizes "supported" as persons in the CPS household.
No equivalence-scale fractional weighting of children/adults.

## D-011 — Second Economic Axis: Survival Floor
**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** ACCEPTED

Add Survival Floor, Survival Gap (`Population Anchor - Survival Floor`), and Adequacy Ratio (`Population Anchor / Survival Floor`) alongside the Population Anchor.
The Survival Floor is modeled from official public baselines (HUD FMR, USDA TFP, EIA RECS, BLS CE, MEPS).
Household survival floors must be independently modeled by composition and economies of scale, never generated by simply multiplying the single-adult floor by household size.
Status remains `RESEARCH ESTIMATE` until validation, independent reproduction, and external benchmark comparisons (MIT Living Wage, ALICE) are complete.

## D-013 — Historical Constant-Dollar Translation
**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** ACCEPTED

Store both nominal dollars and CPI-U adjusted constant 2024 dollars for all historical Population Anchor vintages.
Historical displays default to real constant dollars to prevent nominal inflation from being mistaken for economic improvement.
